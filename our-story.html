Our story

Two friends,
one Fable.

Fable by Kavita Anu began with the passion of two friends who believed Indian wear could feel rooted, expressive and easy to love. Their shared eye for colour, celebration and craft slowly turned into a label made for modern festive wardrobes.

The beginning

Passion became
a wardrobe.

Before Fable became a collection, it was a feeling shared between two friends: the joy of dressing up, the emotion of Indian occasions and the beauty of silhouettes that make someone feel remembered.

That passion became Fable by Kavita Anu - a space where sarees, anarkalis, drapes and festive pieces are imagined with a balance of tradition and contemporary styling. Every look is designed to carry the grace of Indian culture while staying fresh, wearable and personal.

The label celebrates women who want more than an outfit. They want a story: something for weddings, festivals, family celebrations, intimate functions and the small moments that still deserve to feel special.
