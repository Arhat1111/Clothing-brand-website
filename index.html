Our story

Not just clothes.
Stories, stitched.

Fable by Kavita Anu began as the shared passion of two friends who loved Indian dressing, thoughtful styling and clothes that carried emotion.

What started as conversations around colour, craft and celebration slowly became Fable: a wardrobe of sarees, drapes and festive silhouettes made for women who want tradition to feel personal, expressive and current.
