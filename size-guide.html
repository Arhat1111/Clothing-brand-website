Fit reference

Size guide,
made simple.

Use this reference before selecting your blouse, anarkali, drape set or festive silhouette. Indian ethnic wear sizing can vary by cut and fabric, so final fit and alteration advice should be confirmed with the Fable team.

How to use: measure over fitted clothing, keep the tape parallel to the floor and do not pull it too tight. For in-between measurements, choose the larger size and request final alteration guidance.

Women’s ethnic wear

Body measurement
reference chart.

The chart below is a ready-to-wear guide in inches for bust, waist and hip. It is suitable as a practical starting point for Fable saree blouses, anarkalis, kurta sets, drapes and festive separates.

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the bust while keeping the tape straight across the back.

02

Waist

Measure around the natural waistline, usually the narrowest point above the navel.

03

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of the hips, generally 7-8 inches below the waist.

04

Length

For anarkalis, capes and drapes, confirm desired length based on heel height and occasion.

Fit note: This size guide is a general reference only. Structured blouses, heavily embroidered pieces and draped silhouettes may need small alterations depending on body shape, lining and fabric fall.
